Sky & Sedge — Palette Board

An airy, modern-magazine Nature-editorial system for a bird-photography store & portfolio. Photography always leads; UI colors stay quiet and supporting. Where a soft brand color fails accessible text contrast, a deepened accessible variant is added alongside it (soft = large-area/decorative, deep = text/CTA).
